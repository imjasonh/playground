# Clash quick reference

A simultaneous-bidding game for 2 to 4 players. Full rules in [`rules.md`](rules.md).

## What you need

- A standard deck with the four Aces removed: 48 cards in four suits — Intelligence, Strength, Speed, Ingenuity.
- Cards are worth their number; Jack is 11, Queen 12, King 13. The Aces are only Home markers.

## Setup

- 1 to 4 human players fill the rest of the table with bots, for 2 to 4 players total.
- Each player and bot takes a suit — its **Home** — and keeps that Ace face up as a marker.
- Split the shuffled 48 cards evenly, so everyone holds the same stack (24 / 16 / 12 cards at 2 / 3 / 4 players).
- Take 4 cards off your stack into a hand you keep secret; the rest stays face down as your **pile**.
- A bot keeps its whole stack face down as one pile — no hand to look at.

## Each clash

- Everyone turns the top card of their pile face up into the middle. Together these are the **pot**.
- Everyone bids one card at once:
  - humans choose 1 card from their hand and play it face down;
  - bots just turn the next card of their pile and play it.
- Reveal together. Your **bid** is your card's value, plus 1 for each card of your suit already in the pot, plus 1 more if the card you played is your suit.
- The highest bid wins and slides the pot face down under their pile, to come round again. The card you played is spent either way — set it aside; you never win it back and it never returns to your hand.
- A tie for the top wins nothing: leave the pot in the middle and it joins the next clash's pot. It can carry more than once.
- Everyone draws from the top of their pile to refill their hand to 4, and the next clash begins.

## Winning

- Every clash spends one card for good, so the piles shrink. The game ends the moment a player can't play — an empty hand with no pile left to refill it. If only your pile runs out, keep playing from your hand and add nothing to the pot.
- Count the cards you still hold, hand and pile together. The most wins; a tie shares the victory.
