TAIPEI (WINDOWS 1990/95) — AMERICAN MAHJONG PRINT PACK (OPTION A: CENTERED SQUARE)
===================================================================================

Tile Size:       23 x 30 mm (Compact / Standard NMJL American Mahjong tile)
Artwork Layout:  Option A — Pure original 36x36 square centered with all outlines kept
                 - SVG: 18.0 x 18.0 mm centered (0.5 mm/pixel, 2.5 mm side margin, 6.0 mm top/bottom)
                 - 600 DPI PNG: 543 x 709 px canvas, 432 x 432 px (12x exact integer scale) centered

Folders & Files:

    Preferred format for UV flatbed print shops (scales cleanly to 32x23 or 36x25 mm too).
- png_600dpi_30x23mm/
    45 transparent 600 DPI PNGs with exact integer 12x nearest-neighbor scaling (zero blur).

    or cut DIY vinyl stickers).

UV Printing Notes for Shop:
1. Do NOT apply anti-aliasing or smoothing — this is crisp retro pixel art.
2. Print faces with clear UV gloss/matte protective varnish topcoat for shuffle durability.
3. Request a 1-tile test proof (especially Green Dragon & Win95 Joker) to check CMYK neon green/cyan.
