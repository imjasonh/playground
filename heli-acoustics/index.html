Heli acoustics

Put headphones on. This is binaural (HRTF) audio.

A synthesized helicopter orbits you and the browser renders it through an HRTF panner. Turn your head by dragging, and the sound tracks around your ears. The HUD shows measured left and right ear energy so you can see the spatialization, not just assume it. There are no buildings or reflections yet. M0 only proves the direct-sound spatialization is good enough to build on.
