Spin draw

Print a disc, pin it to tracing paper, and trace one numbered line each time you turn it. The lines are mixed around the disc, so the picture stays hard to read until you trace it.

Trace it on paper

  1. Cut out the circle. Printed, it is 170 mm across.
  2. Lay tracing paper on top. Pin both sheets through the center hole. Leave the tracing paper still.
  3. Draw a dot on the tracing paper at the edge of the circle.
  4. Turn the circle so 1 meets the dot. Trace the line with the upright 1.
  5. Turn so the next number meets the dot and trace that line. Continue through the last number.

The picture lands below the pin. Thin paper you can see through works the same way as tracing paper.
