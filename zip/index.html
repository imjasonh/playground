How to play

Draw one path that starts at 1, passes every number in order, and fills every cell. The path moves up, down, left, or right, and it can't cross a thick wall.

Drag from 1 to draw. Drag back along the path, or tap a cell on it, to erase back to that cell. Arrow keys draw one cell at a time, and Backspace erases the last one. Hints point out a wrong turn first, then show the next few cells.
